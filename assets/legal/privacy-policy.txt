PRIVACY AT A GLANCE
Strot uses personal data to provide property, construction, interior, smart-home and property-care services; manage projects, evidence, payments and referrals; connect authorised users with approved service and API partners; and protect the platform. We do not sell personal data. Access is role-based, uploads are kept in private cloud storage, and you may exercise your rights under Nigerian data-protection law.
Design. Build. Live well — with privacy built in.

	•	Introduction and scope
This Privacy Policy explains how Strot Limited ("Strot", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares and otherwise processes personal data when you use our website, portals, mobile or desktop applications, consultations, inspections, project-management tools, payment channels, referral programmes, APIs, artificial-intelligence features, communications or other services (together, the "Services").
It applies to clients and prospective clients; property owners, occupiers and authorised representatives; vendors and service partners; API and technology providers; referral participants; staff and authorised administrators; website visitors; and other individuals whose personal data is processed through the Services.
Where a third-party provider determines independently how it uses personal data—for example, a payment gateway, bank, insurer, mortgage provider or messaging platform—its own privacy notice may also apply.

	•	Personal data we collect
The categories we collect depend on your relationship with Strot and the Services you use.
Identity and profile data. Name, photograph, date of birth where needed, gender where voluntarily supplied, username, account type, identification or verification documents, signature, company and role.
Contact and communication data. Email address, phone and WhatsApp number, postal address, communication preferences, enquiries, support messages, calls, meeting notes and correspondence.
Property and project data. Property address, coordinates, maps, ownership or authority information, land and building details, property type, specifications, drawings, surveys, bills of quantities, work plans, approvals, site instructions, inspections, defects, handover and warranty records.
Media and evidence. Documents, photographs, videos, audio, CCTV or access records where applicable, timestamps, geotags, device metadata, evidence maps and other files uploaded to or created within Strot.
Service and transaction data. Consultations, quotations, orders, contracts, invoices, payment schedules, receipts, Nomba transaction references, bank-transfer details, proof-of-payment files, refunds and payout records.
Partner, provider and workforce data. Business details, qualifications, licences, portfolio, insurance, compliance records, assigned projects, performance, attendance, bank and payout details, agreements and authorised users.
Referral and growth data. Referral link or code, referrer and referred-person identifiers, consent record, attribution data, campaign source, qualifying activity, reward eligibility, payout and anti-fraud checks.
Technical and usage data. IP address, device and browser information, operating system, app version, identifiers, session and security logs, login history, cookie choices, pages or features used, crash and performance data.
Location data. Property or site location and, only where enabled or necessary, device location for inspections, attendance, evidence integrity, navigation, service availability and fraud prevention.
Smart-home and security data. System designs, equipment identifiers, installation and maintenance records, access permissions and operational information. We apply heightened restrictions because this information may reveal security arrangements.
AI interaction data. Prompts, questions, uploaded content, outputs, feedback and related safety or quality signals when you use Strot AI features.
Derived data. Service recommendations, project status, risk indicators, duplicate or quality flags, referral attribution, eligibility assessments and internal location-pricing classifications.
Please do not provide personal data that is not reasonably required for the relevant Service. If you provide another person’s data, you must have authority to do so and, where required, give that person this Policy.

	•	Why we use personal data and our lawful bases
We process personal data only where a lawful basis applies. Depending on the activity, we rely on one or more of the following:
Contract and pre-contract steps. To respond to a request, prepare a quotation, create or administer an account, deliver a Service, manage a project, process a payment or fulfil an agreement.
Legal obligation. To keep required records, respond to regulators or lawful requests, meet tax, accounting, safety, anti-fraud and other compliance duties.
Legitimate interests. To operate, secure and improve Strot; coordinate approved partners; maintain evidence and audit trails; prevent fraud; manage claims; analyse performance; and communicate about related Services, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
Consent. For optional marketing, certain cookies, precise device location, referral invitations, sensitive data or another activity where consent is the appropriate basis.
Vital interests or public interest. Where processing is reasonably necessary to protect someone in an emergency or for a legally recognised public-interest purpose.
Where consent is used, you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out lawfully. If required data is not provided, we may be unable to provide the relevant Service.

	•	AI-assisted features and automated processing
Strot may use artificial intelligence to answer questions, summarise project information, organise evidence, identify missing items, support recommendations, draft communications, assist pricing or risk review and improve workflows. AI outputs may be incomplete or inaccurate and should not replace professional judgement.
Strot does not intend to make a solely automated decision that has a legal or similarly significant effect on you without an appropriate lawful basis, safeguards and a meaningful way to request human review. Material project approvals, payment confirmations, partner decisions and progress certifications remain subject to authorised human oversight.
Do not place passwords, payment-card details, access codes or unnecessary sensitive information in an AI prompt. AI providers receive only the information reasonably needed to provide the feature and are subject to contractual and security controls where applicable.
	•	Property location, maps, media and project evidence
Property and site data may reveal a person’s home, movements, occupancy, assets or security arrangements. We therefore restrict access by project role and purpose. Geotags, timestamps and device information may be used to confirm inspection attendance, evidence integrity, work progress or fraud indicators.
Strot uses an internal location-pricing classification to support consistent commercial decisions. The classification and Strot’s internal margin logic are confidential business information and are visible only to authorised personnel. Clients receive the applicable final price; partners receive only the commercial information necessary for their engagement.
Before uploading media, users should avoid capturing unrelated people, neighbouring properties, identification documents, vehicle plates or private areas unless necessary and lawful. Where individuals are identifiable, the uploader must have an appropriate basis to capture and share the material.
	•	Payments and financial information
Online payments may be processed through Nomba. Nomba and participating banks process payment credentials and transaction data under their own terms and privacy notices. Strot generally receives transaction status, amount, reference and reconciliation information rather than full payment-card details.
For bank transfers, Strot displays account details configured by an authorised Super Admin. A client may upload proof of payment for manual verification. Payment proofs are treated as private project documents and may be reviewed only by authorised finance or administrative personnel. Please obscure unrelated balances, account transactions or personal information before uploading a bank statement or receipt.
A payment is not treated as confirmed solely because a file was uploaded. Strot may verify the transaction with the relevant bank or payment provider and maintain an audit trail of confirmation, rejection, refund or dispute activity.
	•	Referrals and growth programmes
Strot may operate referral arrangements for clients, professional introducers, growth partners, service or API partners and authorised staff. The referral type and eligible opportunities shown in a dashboard depend on the user’s approved role.
Use a referral link or code instead of uploading another person’s details wherever possible.
If you submit a person’s contact details, you must first have their permission to be contacted by Strot and must not misrepresent your relationship with them.
We process referral data to attribute introductions, prevent duplicate or fraudulent claims, determine eligibility, communicate status and make approved reward payments.
A service or API provider cannot refer, view commission for, or earn commission on its own service category. Strot may apply other eligibility, conflict and anti-abuse rules.
Referred individuals may ask Strot to stop contact or delete referral information, subject to legal and anti-fraud retention needs.

	•	How long we retain personal data
We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, including contract performance, safety, warranty, tax, accounting, dispute, fraud-prevention and legal requirements. The periods below are working retention standards and may be extended or shortened where law, a contract, a claim, an investigation or a documented business need requires.
Record category
Typical retention approach
Enquiries and unconverted referrals
Usually 24 months after the last meaningful interaction.
Client account and profile data
While active and generally 6 years after closure or the last transaction.
Contracts, invoices, receipts, payment proofs and reconciliation
Generally 7 years after completion or the relevant financial period.
Partner/provider due diligence and agreements
While active and generally 7 years after the relationship ends.
Project designs, approvals, site evidence, handover and warranty records
Generally 10 years after project completion, or longer for an active warranty, claim, legal duty or agreed archival service.
Support and complaint records
Generally 3 years after closure, longer where connected to a project or dispute.
Security and audit logs
Usually 12–24 months, longer for an investigation or legal hold.
Marketing preferences
Until withdrawal or inactivity; a minimal suppression record may be retained to honour an opt-out.
Deletion from active systems may be followed by a limited backup or versioning period. During that period, data is isolated from ordinary use and removed or overwritten according to the backup cycle. Data subject to a legal hold is retained until the hold ends.
	•	Your data-protection rights
Subject to the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and applicable limitations, you may have the right to:
be informed about the processing of your personal data;
request access to your personal data and information about its use;
request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data;
request deletion where there is no lawful reason to continue processing;
object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing;
request restriction of processing in certain circumstances;
receive eligible data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format or request portability;
withdraw consent at any time where processing relies on consent;
request human intervention and challenge an eligible solely automated decision; and
lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).
To make a request, email hello@getstrot.com with the subject "Privacy Request" and describe the right you wish to exercise. We may verify your identity or authority before acting. We will respond within the period required by applicable law and will explain any lawful limitation, refusal or extension. Ordinarily, no fee is charged, but the law may permit a reasonable fee or refusal for manifestly unfounded or excessive requests.
